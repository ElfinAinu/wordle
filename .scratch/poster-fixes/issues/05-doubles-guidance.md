quotes 14.7% — that figure counts adjacent doubles only. Repeats overall are
32.4% of answers, and 10.3% repeat a vowel apart, which a two-opener fixed
opening cannot detect and the hit-count axis cannot reveal. The panel should
carry the wider figure, distinguish the two kinds plainly, and give one concrete
rule for the invisible kind: to tell one copy of a letter from two, a guess has
to carry that letter twice. Include one worked example the fixed opening is
blind to.
